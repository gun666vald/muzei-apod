A modern, ultra-lightweight NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) wallpaper provider for Muzei Live Wallpaper.

Features:
• Next-Gen NASA Science 4K CDN engine with dynamic upscaling (3840x2160)
• Real-time HTTP 403 Forbidden probe and preview recovery
• Chronological video bypass to guarantee a high-res photo every day
• Zero-allocation socket stream directly into Muzei cache
• Battery-aware scheduling via Android WorkManager
• Ultra-lightweight footprint (372 KB) with zero telemetry or trackers
